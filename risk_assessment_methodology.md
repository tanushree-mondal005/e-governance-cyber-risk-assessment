# Risk Assessment Methodology

This is a qualitative tabletop assessment, informed by NIST CSF 2.0 and NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1. It is not a penetration test, audit certification, or measured probability assessment.

Likelihood: 1 = Low/uncommon; 2 = Medium/plausible; 3 = High/likely or readily attempted.
Impact: 1 = Low/minor; 2 = Medium/noticeable; 3 = High/major service, privacy, fraud, or trust impact.
Score = likelihood × impact. Scores 1–2 are Low, 3–4 Medium, 6 High, and 9 Critical.

Validate system assumptions, data classification, legal obligations, existing controls, and risk scores with the service owner. Reassess after material changes and incidents.
